## Early-module crosswalk

### Module 1 — Intro
Communication themes: offering/accepting drinks, origin, introduction, family, airport, adjective descriptions, ordering, occupations, present-tense use, weather, pets.

Grammar candidates:
- subject pronouns and `to be`;
- `be` in present forms;
- present simple for routines and facts;
- basic adjective position and noun phrases;
- basic questions and sentence order.

### Module 2 — A1
Communication themes: clothes, home, work, feelings, classroom and shopping requests, time, sports, chores, routine, hotel, articles, family, belongings, lost items, city navigation, symptoms, school, safety.

Explicit grammar milestone examples:
- units 3–4: present `to be`, contractions;
- unit 7: present simple for feelings;
- unit 9: time expressions;
- unit 12: frequency adverbs;
- unit 15: articles;
- unit 20: irregular plurals;
- unit 22: present-tense negation;
- unit 24: questions with `to be`;
- units 25 and 27: present continuous and its questions;
- unit 29: affirmative imperatives.

Teaching consequences:
- explain the difference between `be` and `do`;
- teach the third-person `-s`;
- distinguish `be)-questions from `do)-questions;
- show why articles and plurals have to be selected with the noun;
- introduce present continuous only after the simple present is understood.

## Teaching priority for Hungarian learners

When the relevant structures appear, explain explicitly:
- English `to be` cannot normally be omitted from present nominal/adjectival sentences;
- questions/negatives in present and past simple need `do/does/did`, except with `be` and modal verbs;
- articles and zero article differ from Hungarian usage;
- English word order is less flexible and usually needs an overt subject;
- present perfect and progressive aspect do not map one-to-one to Hungarian past/present forms;
- prepositions do not map one-to-one to Hungarian case suffixes;
- countability affects articles and quantifiers;
- gerund/infinitive and phrasal verb meanings cannot be translated mechanically.
